# BA document contract

```markdown
# <change title>

- Type: BUG_FIX | FEATURE | CHANGE
- Status: READY | BLOCKED
- Source request:
- Analyzed revision:

## Business outcome
## Current behavior / process
## Expected behavior / target process
## Actors and affected users
## Scope
## Out of scope
## Business rules
- BR-001:
## Acceptance criteria
- AC-001:
## Exceptions and recovery
## Data, authorization, audit, and availability
## Dependencies and compatibility
## Assumptions and unresolved decisions
## Evidence
## Revision history
```

For bugs, add reproduction steps, frequency, severity, workaround, suspected regression range, and root-cause evidence. For features, add the as-is/to-be process and measurable success condition.
